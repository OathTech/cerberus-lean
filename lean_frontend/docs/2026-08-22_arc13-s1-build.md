### 3.2 The record/replay verdict (the workbench prediction's first real-world test)

**The replay lane was not needed and could not have helped here.**
(1) No stored traces existed to replay: the standing T1-T4 proofs use
plain `app_walk` (they re-walk at every elaboration), and T5 is parked
pre-landing — so the predicted "fingerprint refusal → re-record" path

15x replay-vs-walk thesis (B2 39,985ms vs B3 2,593ms, arc-11) becomes
load-bearing only at T5-walk scale (~75 symbolic rounds); the arc-11
D3 renumbering-compatibility argument (a landed T5 re-records
mechanically via checked replay) remains the plan of record for the
T5-landing arc, now with the datum that the FIXTURE side of a re-pin
