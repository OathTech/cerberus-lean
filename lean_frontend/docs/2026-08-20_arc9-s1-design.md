## 0. Design summary (one screen)

* **Layering**: L0 = iris-lean consumed as-is (post-bump; OwnP adopted,
  ~380 lines of hand-rolled twin retired, §1.1); L1 = the Cerberus
  step layer — six LEMMA KITS at Layer 2 (app-equation land, no Iris),
  extracted from the T1-T4 hand proofs golean-style and generalized to
  COMPUTED-RHS round lemmas (§1.2); L2 = a goal-guarded, law-table
  app-equation walker (`app_walk` quartet) in the golean `go_walk`
  architecture with brick-wp's dispatch discipline (§1.3).
* **The loop rule** (G1, T5's anchor): a PURE Nat-induction composition
  lemma `iter_compose` over fuel-offset app-block equations + a
  per-fixture iteration block proved by the walker; exit and post-loop
  composed OUTSIDE the induction (brick-wp `wp_frame_exit` shape).
  No new axioms; the conclusion is an ordinary app equation consumed by
  the UNCHANGED adequacy chain (§2).
* **Transcription kill**: computed-RHS round lemmas + walker RHS
  synthesis eliminate the intermediate-configuration def layer and the
  hand `.trans` chains; acceptance = golean's form, calibrated by
  re-proving T1's dnms segment (§3).
* **Proof-size bar**: per slate example, the counted proof file ≤ 250
  lines and ≤ 40 manual (non-walker) proof steps, all-semantic;
  measured by a gate script; kit files fixture-free by grep-gate
  (mega-lemma counter) (§4).
* **Slate**: T5 sum-loop, T6 nested loops, T7 array fill+sum, T8 early
  exit, T9 two-function call composition, T10 chvalid cameo (stretch,
  park-clause) (§5).
* All seven parked S0 questions are ANSWERED (§7); one operator-flag
  section (§9) — nothing requiring a stop under the charter's
  autonomy protocol.

### 1.2 L1 — the Cerberus step layer (lemma kits, Layer 2, no Iris)

Provenance tags used below: [IN-HOUSE] = exists arc-7, promoted;
[KIT-EXTRACT] = golean s1/s2 lift-wave pattern (survey §3.3: extract
from consumers, pin, delete duplicates); [BRICK-B<n>] = design lifted
from the survey's §2.1 taxonomy row; [NEW] = build-new per gap-matrix
row cited.

The load-bearing design move (the transcription killer, Q4): round
lemmas are restated with a **COMPUTED RHS** — the post-state is a
FUNCTION of the pre-state and the round's decomposition hypotheses,
never a per-fixture transcribed term. In-tree feasibility evidence
that hypothesis-mediated generic stepping elaborates at default
budgets: `aux2_step`/`aux2_done` (T1AppEq.lean:718-756 — generic in
pe/env/mem/file with `hpull`/`hstep`/`hnv` crossings),
`liftCore_run_defined` (T1AppEq.lean:895-904), `perform_unfold`/
`ars_load_unfold` (T1AppEq.lean:579-600), `eubind_defined`/
`stub_defined` (T1AppEq.lean:648-656), and the whole fuel-generic
bind/lift family (Machine.lean:302-401). What is NOT yet evidenced is
generic reduction through `dnms`'s redex discovery (`get_ctx`) with a
symbolic continuation — S2 probe P1 (§8), fallback: shape-indexed
lemma families (one lemma per arena-context shape, still
fixture-generic).

**Kit/AppEq.lean** [IN-HOUSE, re-export surface] — the monadic spine,
already proved in Machine.lean and left there (it is audited);
this file only re-exports + simp-tags: `app_nd_return`, `app_kill`,
`app_nd_get/put/update/read`, `app_print_debug`, `app_nd_guard_true/false`,
`app_pick_singleton` (Machine.lean:435-482), `app_bind_active/_killed`
(:314/:502), `app_liftND_active/_killed` (:362/:394), `app_ifM`,
`app_addConstraints` (:215/:221).
Contract (all): closed `app`-computation equations, `rfl`-provable,
registered `@[app_eq]` for the walker.

**Kit/Mem.lean** [KIT-EXTRACT + BRICK-B1/B3; gap G4] — the memory-op
block kit at MemState level (pure, no driver types). Extracted from
T1AppEq (allocErr_eq/storeErr_eq/loadX_eq/reconX_eq/roundtrip_arith +
the bmD3 fetch family) and T4AppEq (createS/storeU/storeA/storeB/
loadV/loadA + layout facts), generalized:

* `mem_alloc_block (tid pref align ty addr aid) (hlast : …) (halign : …) :
  app (CerbMem.allocateObject …) mem = (NDactive (mkPtr aid addr), memAfterAlloc mem …)`
  — the fresh-allocation equation with the address/id computed from
  the pre-state fields; named hypothesis args in the brick-B3
  convention (each `h*` a small closed fact the walker discharges by
  `rfl`/`simp [app_norm]`).
* `mem_store_block (ty ptr mval) (hget hbounds hcompat hatomic hbytes) :
  app (CerbMem.storeM …) mem = (NDactive (FP .W a n), memStoreAt mem a bytes)`
  — where `memStoreAt` spells the bytemap in the COLLAPSED canonical
  form (Q3, §7): same-key inserts collapsed, one insert per byte
  address over the canonical base.
* `mem_load_block (ty ptr) (hget hbounds hatomic hdead hbytes hrecon) :
  app (CerbMem.loadM …) mem = (NDactive (FP .R a n, mval), mem)`.
* `bytes_int_roundtrip (w : width) (x) (h1 h2) :
  bytesToInt (intBytes w x) signed = some x` — `roundtrip_arith`
  (T1AppEq.lean:243) generalized over the byte width family actually
  used by the slate (4-byte now; the statement is width-indexed so T7's
  array elements and any future `char`/`long` reuse it).
* `recon_int_block` — `reconX_eq`'s generic form (fuel by the
  literal-successor jump, as now).
* `bytemap_insert_collapse (k v v' m) : (m.insert k v').insert k v = m.insert k v`
  and the `readBytesFrom`-over-inserts fetch lemmas — the TreeMap
  normal-form maintenance family (Q3). At concrete keys these are
  kernel computations; the collapse lemma is the one PROPOSITIONAL
  fact (S2 probe P2 checks whether `Std.TreeMap` gives it structurally
  or via its lawful-map API; either lands in this kit).
* The layout-fact family: `sizeof_fact`/`alignof_fact`/`shift_fact`
  schemas with the `htags` hypothesis convention (T4AppEq.lean:31-67
  pattern — the tag-global hypothesis stays a hypothesis, brick-B13).

Failure behavior (kit-wide): a lemma whose side hypothesis does not
discharge by `rfl`/`app_norm`-simp is NOT applied by the walker — the
hypothesis surfaces as a goal only under explicit `app_walk_step`
(§1.3), mirroring golean's "a tactic that guessed at those would be
guessing at the semantics" (GoWalk.lean:52-56).

### 1.3 L2 — automation (the walker)

Architecture = golean's `go_walk` law-table core (GoWalk.lean:1-124),
retargeted from IPM/WP goals to PURE app-equation goals — the
simplification the survey §3.2 predicted: no resource threading, no
modality dance, no `iframe` renames; the hardest parts of golean's v1
do not exist here. Discipline = brick-wp's measured lessons: dispatch
is GOAL-GUARDED by head shape before any apply (WpTactics.v:2276-2282
— ungated failing applies measured "~4 s per failed attempt and 85% of
wp_auto's total runtime"), and the mechanical/semantic division is
absolute (WpTactics.v:2195-2198: the user provides ONLY semantic
steps).

Components:

* `register_simp_attr app_norm` — THE state normal form (Q3): the
  simp set that maintains the canonical spelling after every step
  (update_env unfolding at concrete patterns, TreeMap insert-collapse
  at concrete keys, `mkDrv`/thread-update record normalization,
  List/trace cons forms). Confluence requirement as golean's
  `go_walk_simp` (GoWalk.lean:130-134). Invariant: every registered
  `@[app_eq]` lemma's LHS and RHS are stated IN this normal form —
  brick-B14's lesson ("the wrong choice makes every kit lemma's LHS
  miss") made structural.
* `@[app_eq]` attribute — registers a kit lemma into a DiscrTree
  keyed on its conclusion's LHS `app <computation> <state>` pattern
  (the golean `lawKeys` move, GoWalk.lean:186-195: metavariable
  telescope so lemma variables index as wildcards). Most-specific-first
  candidate order + per-candidate try under saved state with a small
  budget (golean's two ordering deltas, GoWalk.lean:111-116).
  DELIBERATELY UNREGISTERED (the walk stops in front of them):
  `iter_compose`/`iter_compose_exit` (invariant-carrying — their `St`
  family is a Lean-level ∀-obligation no goal determines,
  GoWalk.lean:59-61), `ccall_compose` (consumes a CalleeBlock the
  human names), and any lemma with a non-mechanical side condition
  (range checks, overflow bounds — G12).
* `app_walk` / `app_walk n` — the loop: match head, apply the one
  registered lemma, discharge its side hypotheses by
  `rfl`/`assumption`/`simp [app_norm]` (and ONLY those), normalize,
  repeat. Goal shape in: `app C σ = R` where R is a metavariable, a
  stated term, or an `app C' σ'` continuation (the `.trans`-chain
  shapes of every AppEq file). Goal shape out: the same equation
  advanced to the first configuration where no registered lemma fires
  — a semantic obligation, an unregistered-rule site, or `rfl`-closable
  (then closed). Failure behavior: stops with the goal untouched by
  the failed attempt, reporting the stuck head shape; never a
  half-applied lemma (golean saved-state discipline, GoWalk.lean:66-69).
  RHS synthesis mode (Q4): when R is a metavariable, each step
  instantiates it through the computed-RHS round lemma + `app_norm`,
  and the final assignment is the synthesized post-state — this is
  what deletes the transcription defs.
* `app_walk_step lem as [x, h, …]` — apply ONE named lemma
  (registered or not) with its semantic hypotheses supplied/named;
  the manual-step primitive the bar counts (§4).
* `app_walk_finish h` — walk until the goal matches the block
  equation `h` supplies, close with `h` (the honest segment end,
  golean GoWalk.lean:45-48).
* `app_walk?` — one step, reporting which lemma fired (golean
  `go_walk1` + brick `wp_step_debug`, WpTactics.v:2366-2374; the B7
  probe-driven-development lesson). Debugging only, banned from
  committed proofs (grep-checked by the size gate, §4).

Meta-code residency rules: the walker is metaprogramming, not
proof-facing semantics (golean's framing, GoWalk.lean:117-123) —
`partial` allowed INSIDE `TacticM` only; every proof it produces is
kernel-checked as usual and swept by the in-build audit; the D14 ban
(no ofReduce* carriers) applies to its outputs and is already enforced
by the axiom gate.

WP-level tactics: NONE this arc (Q6, §7). SlateWP + the L0 rules are
the whole Layer 3 story; iris-lean's `WpGoal`/`runTacticWp`/`iWpPure`
scaffolding (HeapLang/ProofMode.lean:160-186 + the #657 refactor) is
recorded as the METHOD file for the Q4-granularity future, nothing
more.

Derived counts (for the record): L0 ≈ 12 consumed families (table
above); L1 ≈ 45-55 named lemmas across 6 kit files (AppEq ~12
re-exports, Mem ~12, Round ~14, Eval ~9, Loop ~5, CallSpec ~4);
L2 = 5 tactics + 2 attributes.

## 2. The loop story (T5's invariant rule, G1)

Ground truth about the object (pinned `tests/verify/t5_sum.core`,
verified this slice): the C for-loop compiles to a labeled
continuation — `save while_501: unit (i, s) in <guard eval> ; if a_502
then <body ; save __cerb_continue0 … ; run while_501(i, s)> else
pure(Unit)` with `break_500`/`ret_498` labels after. So an iteration
is a FIXED sequence of dnms rounds (guard unseq/loads/compare →
if-split on the guard boolean → body loads/add/store → i-increment
block → `run while_501` jump), and the whole run — prefix, all n
iterations, exit, post-loop, terminal — is still ONE bind-collapsed
`app` computation (arc-7 trace evidence covers T5, Machine.lean
§Coverage-by-need). The loop rule therefore lives at Layer 2, exactly
as the gap matrix concluded: iris-lean has no loop rule at either rev
(survey §1.2, ABSENT), and at one-atomic-WP-step granularity there is
no WP traffic for `iloeb`/fixpoint machinery to carry — the rule is a
Nat-induction over app-block equations.

**The rule, as a contract (Q2 answered: option (a), split into a pure
composition core + walker-proved premises):**

```
-- Kit/Loop.lean, fully generic (no driver types):
iter_compose {α σ : Type} {C : Nat → σ → α} {St : Nat → σ} {k n : Nat}
  (hbody : ∀ i, i < n → ∀ fuel,
     C (fuel + k) (St i) = C fuel (St (i+1))) :
  ∀ fuel, C (fuel + k * n) (St 0) = C fuel (St n)
```

* HYPOTHESES read as the classic triple: `St` is THE INVARIANT (a
  pure, closed-form state family — brick-B9's derivation-indexed
  contract, deliberately kept: for t5, `St i = mkDrv (thLoop i)
  (memLoop (sVal i) i) rs (trL i) (n0 + r*i)` with
  `sVal i = i*(i-1)/2`); `i < n` is THE GUARD's semantic content
  (hbody is provable only while the compiled guard evaluates true —
  the `i < n` hypothesis discharges the comparison chain via
  Kit/Eval's `evalcmp_int_spec`); `hbody` is BODY-PRESERVES (one
  iteration block: k rounds, proved by `app_walk` per fixture,
  ∀-quantified in i and fuel).
* CONCLUSION: the composed k·n-round block equation from `St 0` to
  `St n` — still relative fuel, still an ordinary app equation.
* `k` is the CONSTANT per-iteration round count (constant because the
  body is fixed code; this is what makes Q5's offset algebra
  sufficient, §7).
* Proof obligation of the rule itself: Nat-induction on n over
  equation transitivity — pure Lean, zero axioms; nothing about the
  semantics is assumed. Soundness path: unchanged. The rule
  manufactures an `app`-equation; that equation feeds the UNCHANGED
  chain `wp_of_app_active` → `callHarnessAdequate_of_wp` →
  `wp_adequacy_gen`/`ownP_adequacy` → `callOutcomes_sound` +
  `steps_erased` + `adequate_result` (SlateWP.lean:31-69,
  IrisAdequacy.lean:44-110) — the cone stays exactly [propext,
  runEffectful, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], enforced by the
  in-build audit as today.

**Exit framing (brick-B8, `wp_frame_exit` WpTactics.v:2257-2274
adapted):** the invariant proof never carries the post-loop
continuation. The T5 composition is four separate blocks chained
OUTSIDE the induction:

```
prefix_block ⋯ entry_block          -- create s/i, store 0s, save label: c₀ rounds
  |>.trans (iter_compose … n)        -- k·n rounds, St 0 → St n
  |>.trans exit_block                -- guard false at i = n: c₁ rounds
  |>.trans post_block                -- break/kill/load s/run ret: c₂ rounds → NDactive
```

`exit_block`'s comparison discharges from `¬(n < n)`; early-exit slate
entries (T8) replace `iter_compose` with `iter_compose_exit`:

```
iter_compose_exit … (hbody : ∀ i, i < j → …) (hexit : exit-block at St j) :
  -- j ≤ n the exit index; the family only covers 0..j
```

**Fuel instantiation (G2/Q5):** the round lemmas are already
fuel-generic (`fuel+1` shapes throughout T1AppEq/Machine); only the
final instantiation at the executable's `lemDefaultFuel` needs
arithmetic:

```
fuel_split_at_default (c : Nat) (h : c ≤ lemDefaultFuel) :
  dnms lemDefaultFuel acc tids = dnms ((lemDefaultFuel - c) + c) acc tids
```

by `congrArg` + `Nat.sub_add_cancel` — with `c = c₀ + k*n + c₁ + c₂`
and slate bounds n ≤ 100, k ≲ 30, `c ≤ ~3,100 ≪ 999,999` discharges by
`omega`/`decide` on the `n ≤ 100` hypothesis. No fuel-monotonicity
family is needed (§7 Q5). The same split handles the driver2/nd_bind
fuels via the existing wrapper-defeq hooks (FuelHooks.lean — cited,
never unfolded).

**The T5 statement** (fixed now so S2 states it verbatim): identical
form to T1-T4 —

```
T5Statement : ∀ n : Int, 0 ≤ n ∧ n ≤ 100 →
  CallHarnessAdequate t5File.tagDefs t5File "sum" [intValue n] t5Fs
    (fun r => r.dres_core_value = intValue (n * (n - 1) / 2))
```

interpreter-only (CallHarnessAdequate = the CerbND-shaped headline,
Call.lean:322), plus the `T5_ubFree`/`T5Outcomes` faces as T1
(T1.lean:152-171). Statement-TCB gate untouched. NOTE the closed form:
sum of 0..n-1 = n(n-1)/2 (the fixture's loop adds i for i < n); the
`sVal` family's side conditions (`sVal i` in int range for i ≤ 100)
are omega-trivial at these bounds — the stack-ceiling register item
(~1.5k iterations) stays out of scope as chartered.

## 4. The proof-size bar (final)

convention made concrete:

250/40; additionally FAIL on: `app_walk?` in any committed proof
file, and any fixture-symbol reference (`t[0-9]+File`, fixture sym
names) inside `Kit/*.lean` — the MEGA-LEMMA COUNTER, mechanized: a
"kit lemma" that names a fixture is bar-gaming by construction. The

SAY); the script is its mechanical floor. Tallies land verbatim in

**The grammar** (Lean structures/inductives, meta-level only — never
proof-facing; 12 node kinds + 5 enumerations, ~26 constructors
total):

```
WalkTrace     := { goal : GoalKey, fp : Fingerprint,
                   rounds : Array RoundTrace, outcome : Outcome }
Fingerprint   := { kit : UInt64,        -- hash of the sorted @[app_eq]
                                        -- registry (name, keys, prio,
                                        -- statement hash)
                   gen : UInt64,        -- generated-tree pin (the
                                        -- fork-drift layer-2 hash set
                                        -- + lake-manifest revs)
                   fixture : UInt64,    -- emit-lean-core drift hash
                   engine : Nat }       -- AppWalk engineRev (new
                                        -- constant, bumped on any
                                        -- behavioral engine change)
RoundTrace    := { idx : Nat, lhsKeys : Array DiscrTree.Key,
                   cands : Array CandTrace,
                   fired : Option FiredTrace, ledger : Ledger }
CandTrace     := { law : Name, prio : Nat, fate : CandFate }
CandFate      ::= lhsMismatch | hypFailed (i : Nat) (r : Residual)
                | residualMvars | fired
FiredTrace    := { law : Name, args : Array ArgRef,
                   hyps : Array HypTrace,
                   boundary : Option BoundaryTrace,
                   sealedRound : Option Name }
HypTrace      ::= computed (lane : NormLane) (seals : Array SealEv)

                    (pos : Bool) (cert : CertRef)

                | rflClose (cert : CertRef)
```

paths, indices, and depth/time numbers — never full `Expr`s. Large

pretty-key) references; `GoalKey` is the goal's DiscrTree key path +
a statement hash.

+ `SealEv`; law selection + candidates considered → `RoundTrace.cands`
with `CandFate` (this is the survey rank-3 trace requirement, fed
from day one); instantiated arguments → `FiredTrace.args`; semantic

`HypTrace.scalar.leafAuxes` + `decideFacts`; ledger events →

### 12.2 The replay contract (preview / checked replay / stability / metrics)

**The trace is UNTRUSTED DATA.** Soundness never rests on a trace: a
wrong or stale trace can only cause loud failure, never a wrong
theorem, because replay re-runs every kernel check the discovery walk
runs (the VST-A untrusted-plan-checker pattern and Islaris's checked
trace simplification, per the survey §3.1/rank 4 — the plan guides,
the checker decides).

**Preview mode (`app_walk_preview`, NEVER CI-authoritative).**
Discovery without certificates: the preview driver folds over an
explicit `(currentLhs : Expr)` — it does NOT operate on the goal
metavariable. Per round it runs the identical dispatch + hypothesis
CHECKS (DiscrTree query, LHS unification, value computation via the
same normalization lanes, `Kernel.isDefEq`/`Kernel.whnf` checks that
discovery would certify), records the trace, and takes the law's
instantiated RHS as the next `currentLhs`. It never calls
`goal.assign`/`mvar.assign`-on-proof-positions/`mkAuxRfl`/
`mkAuxTheorem`/`addDecl` — no proof term and no declaration is ever
constructed. As a tactic it ALWAYS FAILS after reporting (throws with
the trace summary), so a proof containing it cannot elaborate.

*The negative test (designed now, lands with the feature —
S1 batch 2):* three enforcement levels, each independently
sufficient:

1. **The discriminating pair (test/Unit/AppWalkTest.lean, new E7):**
   the SAME contract-table goal that E1 closes with `app_walk` is
   stated again with `app_walk_preview` under `#guard_msgs` pinning
   the exact failure ("preview only — goal intentionally left
   unsolved" + the unsolved-goals error). Capability present in the
   walk, provably absent in preview, on the same goal. (Labeled a
   TEST — untrusted-evaluator checking per doctrine; the trust
   argument is levels 2-3.)
2. **The gate ban:** `scripts/check_proof_size.sh`'s debug-surface
   ban extends to `app_walk_preview` in tracked relsem files
   (plant-tested both directions, the A-F5 recipe) — no CI-accepted
   theorem can even invoke it.
3. **The structural property (S5 audit scope (a)):** the preview
   driver's call graph contains no assignment/declaration site
   (`goal.assign`, `h.assign`, `addDecl`, `mkAuxTheorem`,
   `mkAuxDefinition` unreachable from it) — auditable by reading one
   function; recorded as an explicit audit obligation.

**Checked replay (`app_walk_replay`).** Input: a `WalkTrace`. Per
recorded round: skip the candidate SEARCH (no DiscrTree query, no
candidate loop, no assumption scan), instantiate the recorded law,
discharge each hypothesis by its recorded `HypTrace` lane (the
recorded fact NAME for assumptions; the recorded normalization lane
for computed values; the recorded decide-fact for chases), and emit
certificates through the EXISTING per-stage emitter — the same
`mkAuxRfl`/`mkStateBridge`/`sealCtorLeaves`/round-seal code paths,
producing ordinary kernel-checked declarations indistinguishable in
kind from a discovery walk's. Replay VERIFIES as it goes: every
`isDefEq`/kernel check runs exactly as in discovery — the trace
removes CHOICE, not checking. On any divergence (law no longer
unifies, fact missing, lane fails): loud error naming the trace step
and reason — NO fallback to search (determinism preserved; the user
re-runs discovery explicitly). The existing axiom-cone, statement,
and proof-size gates apply to replay outputs unchanged (the survey
rank-1 acceptance gate).

**Trace stability (what invalidates a trace).** `Fingerprint`
mismatch ⇒ replay refuses up front: (a) any change to the registered
law surface (kit hash: names/keys/priorities/statement hashes of the
`appEqExt` registry, computed at record and replay time); (b)
generated-code movement (the fork-drift layer-2 hash set + LemLib/
iris manifest revs — already pinned by standing gates, reused); (c)
fixture drift (the emit-lean-core hash); (d) engine behavior change
(`engineRev`, bumped manually — a forgotten bump can only produce a
loud mid-replay failure, never unsoundness, because of the
untrusted-data property). Fingerprints are a freshness UX, not a
trust surface.

**The three separated metrics** (survey rank-1 acceptance gate;
Islaris's automation-vs-Qed-time reporting discipline):

| metric | what | where measured |
|---|---|---|
| discovery | search + normalization + kernel-defeq checks, no certificates | preview wall time; per-round in `RoundTrace.ledger` (ms + heartbeats — the F-S3-6 ledger, now recorded) |
| elaboration | proof-term assembly + elaborator-side aux closure | replay tactic wall MINUS the summed certificate segments (timers already exist at the emitter call sites; they populate `CertRef` rows) |
| kernel | certificate checking | (i) per-aux `addDecl` wall (the raw-addDecl path is pure kernel time); (ii) the declaration-end check, reported as whole-decl wall minus tactic wall and LABELED DERIVED |

Instrument: a bench probe (Lithium review §6 item 7) timing the T1
calibration, the entry block, and one iteration round in

### 12.3 Context-query extension to `@[app_eq]` (survey rank 3) + typed residuals

**Problem evidence (local):** (a) the S3 `addrOpt` DiscrTree-miss
class — a dead-position literal in a law key silently emptied
dispatch (arc-9 errata); (b) the loop-back park — "an earlier
candidate plants a raw match-arm through ctor-spine unification; the
selection-first ordering avoids it only when the selection
certificate lands" (S3 §8.3): law CHOICE needed context the goal head
does not carry; (c) A-F6 — engine behavior coupled to name
conventions where structured metadata should rule.

**The metadata extension.** `@[app_eq]` gains an OPTIONAL per-premise
required-fact declaration:

```
@[app_eq (fact := hget 3)]        -- premise binder `hget` is
                                  -- context-queried, keyed by its
                                  -- LHS head + argument position 3
                                  -- (the address/identifier slot)
theorem mem_load_block … (hget : mem.allocations.get? allocId = some alloc) …
```

Registration computes and stores `(binder, head : Name, keyPos)`.
Walk-time semantics, per candidate law carrying required facts, run
BEFORE committing to the law (applicability, not post-hoc
discharge):

1. Unify the law LHS with the goal LHS (as today), partially
   instantiating the premise's type.
2. The premise's LHS head must be a constant `H` and its key argument
   (position `keyPos`) must be metavariable-free; otherwise the law
   is INAPPLICABLE this round (recorded `hypFailed`, class
   `missingLaw`-adjacent — never a guess).
3. Query the local context DETERMINISTICALLY: newest-first (Lean
   lctx order reversed — the same order Lithium's `liFindHyp`
   Esnoc-recursion realizes), hypotheses whose type is an equation
   with LHS head `H`; the KEY argument is compared first
   (syntactic fast path, then budget-capped defeq) — non-matching
   keys are skipped WITHOUT full-type unification (today's op
   pre-match generalized from the hardcoded `RelSem.app` position to
   declared keys).
4. FIRST key-match COMMITS: one full-type defeq check
   (budget-capped, ledgered). Success ⇒ the fact discharges the
   premise (no re-search in `dischargeHyp`). Failure ⇒ the law is
   inapplicable, residual class `defeqBridge`, and NO further
   scanning — committed choice, no backtracking.

**Determinism + ambiguity (survey rank-3 acceptance gate:
"Ambiguity is an error unless resolved by explicit priority").**

* STATIC: registration-time check — two laws with IDENTICAL
  DiscrTree key paths AND equal priority is an attribute ERROR naming
  both (extends `appEqKeysOfDecl`'s loud-failure style;
  AppEqAttr.lean:60-62). Recorded limit: identical-keys is a
  conservative overlap approximation; semantic overlap is not
  decidable — the dynamic trace covers practice.
* DYNAMIC: within a round, if two candidates at the SAME priority
  both pass their applicability gates (LHS + required facts), the
  walk ERRORS (ambiguousLaw residual, both names) instead of
  first-wins. Cheap: the gate check, not full discharge; equal-prio
  collisions are already rare by depth-priority.
* TRACE: `RoundTrace.cands` always records every candidate considered
  and its fate — the full candidate trace the acceptance gate
  demands, in preview, discovery, and replay alike.

**The typed residual protocol.** Every walk stop and every
non-mechanical premise is CLASSIFIED (the `Residual` enum, §12.1),
replacing today's unstructured stop:

* `semantic` — an app-shaped crossing no registered law fires on
  (today's laws-only STOP, AppWalk.lean:1019-1027): human
  `app_walk_step` territory, as always (G12 unchanged).
* `arithmetic` — a pure residual with arithmetic head (≤/</= /decide
  over Int/Nat): surfaced with its class so the future post-walk
  batch solver (Lithium review §6 item 3, T6-T7 material) has a
  landing site; NOT auto-solved in v2 beyond today's omega-at-leaves.
* `defeqBridge` — computed-value `isDefEq` failed with agreeing
  heads: a normalization/spelling divergence (the S3 hfind class) —
  actionable as an engine/normal-form bug, now distinguishable from
  missing laws.
* `ambiguousLaw` — as above; an ERROR, not a residual to work around.
* `missingLaw` — empty candidate set on an app-shaped position,
  reported WITH the computed DiscrTree key path (the EMPTY-CANDS
  debug dump at AppWalk.lean:967-975, promoted to structured data) —
  the kit-extension work order format for the S3
  stop-extract-redo loop.

**Registration linter** (Lithium review §6 item 6): at attribute
time, warn on dead-position literals in law keys (the `addrOpt` bug
class: a literal key position whose argument the law body ignores)
and on required-fact keys that can never be ground at walk time.

**Evidence map (design choice → source):**

| choice | evidence |
|---|---|
| context query as APPLICABILITY, keyed + deterministic, committed once | Lithium `FindInContext`/`FindHypEqual` + `once`-committed context search (lithium review §1.2 item 2; definitions.v:158-182, interpreter.v:537-592) |
| key = address/identifier argument, not full unification | Islaris `findR(r)`/`findM(a)` — register/address-keyed deterministic lookup replacing nondeterministic resource choice (survey §3.1 [P]) |
| required-fact as part of the rule format, from goal + logical state | Diaframe 2.0 rule formats with "optionally required logical state" (survey §3.1 [P]); near-applicability under connectives explicitly NOT imported yet (survey rank-3: "only after the simple context query") |
| typed residual inventory, no silent broad solver | Lithium side-condition shelving + declared safe/unsafe classes (review §1.4; simpl_classes.v:18-31) — ours stays harder: surface, never batch-solve silently in v2 |
| full candidate trace + ambiguity-is-error | survey rank-3 acceptance gate verbatim; Lithium's structural determinism (§1.2 [R]) made checkable instead of assumed |

### 12.4 Footprint-shaped premises (Lithium review push) — the call: incremental IN, wholesale DEFERRED

**The push** (review §7): condition laws on lookup/accessor equations
rather than whole-state values, so whole-state defeq questions
disappear from segment boundaries rather than being decomposed after
the fact.

**Where the codebase already is:** premises are LARGELY
footprint-shaped today — `mem_load_block`'s hget/hbounds/hbytes are
`get?`/`readBytesFrom` equations (Kit/Mem.lean:92-107), the
T5Prefix fact family (`bmL_get_*`, `readN/I/S_t5`,
`loadN/I/S_t5`, `storeS/I_t5`) is exactly lookup-conditioned, and
Kit/Map is the lookup-law engine. What remains whole-state: law
CONCLUSIONS (computed-RHS post-states), the walk's goal states, the
St family members, and the statement boundary.

**Honest pricing from the 44/79 evidence.** What blocks rounds
45-79? Re-verified this slice (§12.0 item 4 + the census): the stuck
region's round classes are (i) ONE missing law class — the
continuation-lambda advance (§12.5); (ii) the Erun/Esave loop-back
jump class (`round_run_jump`, designed §1.2, not yet built — Kit/
Round has no Erun-jump law); (iii) discovery latency (~1 h/round in
the arc-9 session; the engine mechanics now reproduce at
60-350 ms/round warm — the hour was DIAGNOSIS, not machine time).
The remaining memory-op rounds (R76 load, R83 NEG, R87 store) are
classes the walk already clears earlier in the same iteration. Zero
of the S3 walls that remain open are whole-state-defeq walls — every
one of those fell to the emitter (S3 §8.4: decompose + kernel engine
+ local budgets). Footprint-restating the laws adds no missing law
and does not shorten diagnosis; trace/replay + the two named laws
attack exactly (i)-(iii). The counter-case (footprint as the cheaper
T5 path) would hold if the remaining rounds each demanded bespoke
whole-state facts — they demonstrably do not (five family facts
cover all seven iteration memory ops).

**The call:**

* **IN (v2, rides S1/S2 at price S):** (a) every NEW kit law
  (including §12.5's) states its premises as accessor/lookup
  equations wherever a whole-state premise is not forced — the
  existing house style, now written down as a rule; (b) Kit/Map's
  lookup layer extends to the labeled-continuation map
  (`find_labeled_continuation`/`rsD5.labeled` lookups — the
  loop-back region needs exactly this); (c) whole-state values are
  confined to statement boundaries, St-family members, and law
  conclusions.
* **DEFERRED (wholesale conversion, price L):** restating round/mem
  law CONCLUSIONS as lookup-characterizations is a new judgment
  shape — it reworks the walker's goal language, the St families,
  and adds a per-theorem conversion proof at the statement boundary
  (statements must remain concrete-state app equations — the TCB
  shape is not negotiable). The Lithium regime that motivates it
  (no concrete states in goals) is structurally theirs, not ours
  (review §2.2 [R]: their heap is ghost state; our theorems are
  about an executable semantics). RE-PRICE TRIGGER: T7 (arrays) —
  the first slate entry whose St family carries symbolic-valued
  cells at scale; if its segment boundaries measure a NEW
  whole-state-defeq wall class that the per-stage emitter does not
  clear, the conversion is re-priced there with that evidence.

### 12.5 The continuation-lambda advance law (the named resumption point) — design sketch

**The measured shape** (S3 §8.3, position re-validated §12.0):
at the stuck round the next unknown is
`advance_step tagDefs tid (fun p => …)` — a step whose payload
position carries a raw continuation LAMBDA (planted when an earlier
candidate's ctor-spine unification exposes a match-arm), which no
DiscrTree law key can index (lambda-headed payloads key as
wildcards/mismatches against the ctor-headed law set).

**Two-pronged design (engine first, law second):**

1. **Engine prong (may dissolve the class):** the trace instrument
   (§12.1) on the stuck round shows WHERE the lambda-headed step
   enters (which `HypTrace.computed` lane planted it and under which
   `NormLane`). If it is a normalization artifact — a step value left
   un-whnf'd where `sealCtorLeaves`'s
   kernel-whnf-before-classify (AppWalk.lean:602-607) would expose
   the ctor — the fix is engine-side (extend the
   normalize-before-plant invariant to the selection lane) and NO new
   law is needed. This is checked FIRST in S2 (one probe under the
   trace; cheap).
2. **Law prong (the named Kit law, if the shape is genuine):**
   `advance_runstate_tau_cont` (Kit/Round, T4 stuck-form
   discipline): generic over the continuation `k` and its argument
   `v`, with the applied continuation as an ordinary premise —

   ```
   advance_runstate_tau_cont
     {k : <arg> → core_runM thread_state} {v : <arg>} …
     (hm : k v σ.core_run_state0 = Result (Defined th', rs')) :
     app (advance_step tagDefs tid
           (Step_with_runstate2 (RSK_tau dbg TSK_Misc) (k v))) σ
       = (NDactive NOWAKEUP, dnmsBump tid th' {σ with core_run_state0 := rs'})
   ```

   i.e. `advance_runstate_tau_misc` (Kit/Round.lean:154) with the
   step-monad argument in APPLIED-lambda form; `hm`'s beta-redex LHS
   goes through the computed-value lane (kernel-cheap). The exact
   binder/annotation shape is FIXED BY THE S2 PROBE under the trace
   instrument, not guessed here (park-don't-improvise; the S3 record
   warns the shape appears through unification, so its spelling must
   be read off the real goal). DiscrTree key: the `Step_*` ctor with
   a wildcard payload — applicability disciplined by a required-fact
   key (§12.3), making this law the context-query extension's first
   client. Registered `@[app_eq]` only if its premises are
   mechanical by the G12 division; otherwise it stays an
   `app_walk_step` law like the NEG draw laws.
3. **Companion (same region):** `round_run_jump` — the §1.2-designed
   Erun/Esave jump law (labeled-continuation lookup + parameter
   rebinding), premised on a `find_labeled_continuation … = some
   (params, body)` LOOKUP equation (the §12.4-IN style) discharged
   through the extended Kit/Map layer at the pinned `rsD5.labeled`.
