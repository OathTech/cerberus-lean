### 3.6 golean's sharpest lesson (one line)

Proof cost is the binding constraint and it is paid down in a fixed
order: extract lemma KITS from the hand proofs first, then a
law-table walker whose acceptance is "same statement, same axiom set,
~an order of magnitude fewer lines", and only reach for a symbolic
evaluator when the KERNEL (not the human) becomes the bottleneck —
with every step gated by measured cost, never by hope.
