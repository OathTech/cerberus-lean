### Walker findings of record (implementation deltas vs the design)

* **dnms factorization** (§2 above): round CLASSES live at the
  `advance_*` level + per-round `step_ctx` discovery facts, not as
  per-class dnms wrappers.
* **`hfuel` wildcard pattern**: `@[app_eq]` laws put a bare variable
  in the fuel position + an `hfuel : fuelS = fuel + k` hypothesis
  discharged by Nat-offset unification, so one law covers literal and
  symbolic fuels (DiscrTree cannot match `999999` against `?f + 1`).
* **Spine normalizer** (`normSpine`): lazy unification assigns
  computed values (step_ctx results) as unreduced redexes invisible to
  DiscrTree keys; the walker normalizes computed-value hypotheses
  (`lhs = ?m` with bare-mvar RHS) to bounded-depth constructor spines
  before matching.
* **App-crossings are LAWS ONLY**: the raw-`rfl` fallback is banned
  for `app`-shaped side hypotheses — a raw-whnf'd crossing (measured
  at the T1 load round) synthesizes junk states that defeat dispatch
  and blow term-size; the walker STOPS instead (the semantic/
  mechanical division, mechanized).
* **Budget accounting** (heartbeat doctrine compliance): each walker
  ROUND and each `app_walk_step` runs in a fresh heartbeat window
  CAPPED at (never above) the ambient per-declaration budget —
  accounting parity with the one-declaration-per-round arc-7 style
  this replaces; per-candidate attempts run under a 50k-heartbeat
  sub-cap with runtime exceptions caught (fail-fast, golean
  GoWalk discipline). No maxHeartbeats is ever RAISED anywhere.
* **Chain-first `app_walk_step`**: `exact`-first would unify a step's
  RHS against the goal's terminal RHS = a whole-remaining-run
  reduction grind (measured: alone trips the budget on the T1 chain).
* **Kernel deep-recursion finding**: carrying the
  `advance_step`-branch defeq through implicit unification sends the
  KERNEL into deep recursion; the pinned explicit equation
  (`advance_action_unfold` + `rw`) keeps the check linear.
