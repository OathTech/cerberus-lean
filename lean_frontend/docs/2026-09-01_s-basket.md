## 2. Item notes (root causes, adjudications, findings)

### 2.1 pr44468 — the real defect was dropped tagDefs THREADING

The CI sweep recorded `PANIC at CerbMem.offsetsof: unknown tag` on
`torture_success/pr44468.c` and priced it as a "tag-environment
population gap". Root cause is sharper: OCaml's layout family threads
`~tagDefs` through every recursive call
(impl_mem.ml:111-119,150,160,168-169,214,226,229) with
`?(tagDefs= Tags.tagDefs ())` optional-arg defaults; the Lean mirror
had dropped the threading and read the ambient global at struct arms.
The AilEoffsetof constant fold (translation.lem:2730 →
`Mem.offsetof_ival`) runs at ELABORATION time, before main.ml:304-305
populates the global — so `offsetof` on a struct with a struct-typed
member (the inner sizeof/alignof needs the tag environment) panicked.
Minimal reproducer (both modes, 6 lines): `struct S {int i;};
struct R {struct S a;}; offsetof(struct R, a)`.

Fix mirrors the OCaml threading exactly; the wrappers default to the
global like the OCaml optional arg; the threaded value is the Fmap
with spine conversion only at lookup arms (leaf-type sizeof/alignof
pay nothing new — no hot-path regression). Post-fix, pr44468 is
byte-identical to the oracle (`Defined {value: "Specified(0)", …}`).

**Upstream finding (tray candidate, [AGENT] probe 2026-09-01):** the
Union arms of both sizeof and alignof read the GLOBAL
(`Pmap.find tag_sym (Tags.tagDefs ())`, impl_mem.ml:173, :255) even
though their member folds thread `~tagDefs`. Live probe: the ORACLE
crashes (exit 125, `Failure("Tags definitions must be set…")`) on
elaboration-time offsetof over a union-containing struct. Mirrored
deliberately (documented in-code); pinned as a CRASH PAIR
(`tests/immaculate/nolibc/offsetof-union-member.c` — either side
starting to succeed is a divergence finding). Registered in TODO.md.

Pins: `tests/immaculate/libc/pr44468.c` (the named reproducer, +
vendored cerberus.h for its quoted include) MATCH Specified(0);
`nolibc/offsetof-nested-struct.c` MATCH Specified(0);
`nolibc/offsetof-union-member.c` MATCH (CRASH pair).
