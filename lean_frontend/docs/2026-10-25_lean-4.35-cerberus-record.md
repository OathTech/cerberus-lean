did, `trimAscii.toInt?` parses `"12"`, `" -3 "`, refuses `"x"`.

**(b) `split` on the `lem_if` guard in the measure proofs** —
`Utils_lemMeasureProofs.lean:42,69,96` and `Defacto_memory_lemMeasureProofs.lean:78,142`.
Measured cause (sharper than the scoping's phrasing): after
`simp only [<worker>_lemFuel, intLtb]` the goal reads
`@ite _ (decide (i < n) = true) (instDecidableEqBool (intLtb i n) true) …` — simp
rewrote the condition but not the `Decidable` instance inside the `ite`, so the goal
is not type-correct under implicit transparency and 4.35.0's `split` (and `cases h :`)
fail at their `generalize` step (`Tactic 'split' failed: Could not split an 'if' or
'match' expression in the goal`; 4.32.2's `split` tolerated it). Repair, the smallest
diff: the comparison helper (`intLtb`/`intLteb`/`natGtb`/`natGteb`/`intGteb`) is no
longer unfolded by the `simp only`, `split` works on `if intLtb i n = true`, and the
bridge gets its type (`have h' : i < n := of_decide_eq_true h`; the Defacto sites'
`fun hle => h (decide_eq_true hle)` elaborates unchanged). Probed first on a scratch
copy of the Utils theorem: axioms `[propext, Quot.sound]` (a `by_cases` variant was
rejected for the risk of `Classical.choice` entering the obligation cones).
